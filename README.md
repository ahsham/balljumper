# 🎌 Jumping Ball Runner — Bot Rival Mode

A twist on the classic Chrome Dino Game.  
But here, you’re not just running against obstacles — you’re racing against a **bot rival**.

⚡ Build **Flow**, grab boosts, hit NOS, and stay ahead. Every clean jump accelerates you forward — every mistake costs you time, lives, and lets the bot surge past.

---

## 🎮 Features
- **Bot Rival Mode** — the bot rubber-bands dynamically. Fall behind and it pushes you, get sloppy and it shoves you back.  
- **Flow mechanic** — play clean and your speed auto-boosts up to +35%.  
- **Boost pickups** — collect ⚡ boosts for +35% speed bursts.  
- **NOS (Nitro)** — hold → or D for a temporary speed burst, drains then refills.  
- **Shields, Hearts & Coins** — smash obstacles, gain extra lives, rack up points.  
- **Progression** — Levels, best scores, and lives are saved locally.  
- **Juicy feedback** — particle effects, sound beeps, rubber-band rival, and toast messages keep the run exciting.  

---

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Jump:** `Space`, `↑`, or **Tap/Click**  
- **NOS Burst:** Hold `→` or `D`  
- **Pause:** `P`  
- **Retry:** `R`  
- **Mute/Unmute:** `M`  

---

## 🚀 How to Play
1. Open `g1.html` in your browser.  
2. Press **Play**.  
3. Jump, dodge, collect, and **outrun the bot**.
